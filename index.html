One group, three pillars

The innovation engine of Constructor Group.

Constructor Knowledge is part of Constructor Group — an all-in-one platform for research and education. This broader ecosystem includes Constructor Tech, which drives technological innovation and product development in AI, data science, digital learning, and research infrastructure. Constructor Knowledge supports Constructor Tech with essential educational content, human capital, research facilities, and test environments, forming the innovation engine of the Group. Constructor Capital is the third main pillar of the group - a science-driven venture capital arm that invests in early-stage companies across deep tech, software, and EdTech. Provides capital, mentorship, and access to the Constructor innovation ecosystem to support scalable innovation and investment opportunities.
