Pillars & Approach

Constructor Knowledge is a unique full-spectrum ecosystem — from early education and research, to tech innovation, investment, and infrastructure. We, at Constructor Knowledge are constructing the future of education, research and professional growth.

Germany’s #1 international university

Academic excellence at Constructor University

Located in Bremen, Constructor University sets the gold standard for private international education in Germany — cultivating global citizens through academic rigor and innovation.

Inclusive & online learning

Global & accessible education

Powered by Nexford USA, our global tech and business programs deliver cutting-edge education anytime, anywhere — breaking barriers worldwide.

Early & youth education

Lifelong learning foundation

From Calcularis for Kids to Talent School for Teens, we nurture curiosity and foundational skills, cultivating the innovators of tomorrow.

Europe & Latin America

Professional academies & schools

Constructor Nexademy in Switzerland and Germany, and EBAC across Brazil, Mexico, Colombia and Peru, cover the full span of applied skills — from data science, programming and generative AI to digital transformation, and on to design, graphics and 3D modelling.

Continuous labs & accelerator program

Research, innovation & startup acceleration

Our Constructor Labs and Constructor Start - an accelerator program, unite researchers, industry leaders, and startups — driving breakthroughs, scalable innovation and tech entrepreneurship 

Investment & real estate development

Capital & infrastructure ecosystem

  • Constructor Capital: Science-driven venture capital investing in deep tech, software, and EdTech startups.
  • Constructor Campus: The real estate arm managing and developing education infrastructure.
Advanced technology & digital solutions

Constructor Tech — the innovation engine

Constructor Tech leads the development and integration of AI, data-powered platforms, and cutting-edge digital tools that empower education, research, and industry transformation across our ecosystem.
